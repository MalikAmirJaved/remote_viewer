Windows clumsy presets (manual)
===============================

Install: https://jagt.github.io/clumsy/

lan:        no filtering
wan-good:   lag 20 ms (one-way), drop 0.1%, throttle ~20 Mbps if available
wan-weak:   lag 75 ms, drop 3%, throttle ~1 Mbps
mobile-3g:  lag 150 ms, drop 5%, throttle ~400 kbps
lossy-wifi: lag 30 ms, lag variance/jitter ~40 ms, drop 8%, throttle ~5 Mbps

Filter inbound+outbound UDP for the agent/viewer ports under test.
Always stop clumsy after the run.
